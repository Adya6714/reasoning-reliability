## Research question and claim

The paper proposes evaluating a task family with a parameterized generator and matched answer-preserving presentations, rather than a static accuracy score.

## Method

Generated ordering tasks vary scale and presentation. The paper explicitly notes that success over a finite tested region does not certify exhaustive competence, and behavioral failures do not locate the internal cause.

## Relevance

Supports our family-based paired design and the boundary between behavioral measurement and mechanistic explanation. Our task families and intervention axes must remain independently specified; this paper is not evidence that any particular surface transform diagnoses retrieval.
