# Reasoning Reliability

## Current question

When do language models preserve correct behavior across equivalent task presentations, and which training, inference, or system conditions explain failures?

## Decision rule

Behavioral invariance is the measured construct. Retrieval/computation labels require validation against randomized known exposure and causal evidence. A model's rationale is not ground truth about its internal process.

## Filing new papers

Use [[templates/PAPER_NOTE]] for each paper. Put metadata and stable links in the note; manage downloaded PDFs in Zotero and link the Zotero item. Do not copy PDFs into Git unless the license explicitly permits redistribution. Add a one-line entry to [[06_Literature_Index]].
