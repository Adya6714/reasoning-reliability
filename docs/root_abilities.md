# Root abilities and ladders

- The 17 failure types in `research-vault/09_Test_Menu_and_Selection.md` collapse into 8 root abilities (break a 12th-grade skill into 8th-grade skills).
- Each ability gets a 4-level ladder. A model's profile = its breaking point on each ladder (for example state horizon, lookahead horizon, hop horizon).
- v1 probes covered only 3 of the 8.
- Mapping below is a DRAFT; check it against the 17-type table and flag mismatches.

| ID | Ability (plain) | Failure types it explains (numbers from 09) | Human theory | L1 atomic example | Profile measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Keep track of what changed | 1 loses context/state | working memory updating (Miyake) | coin under cup 1, swap cups 1 and 2, where is it? | state horizon (updates before failure) |
| R2 | Know what "done" means and check it | 5 stops early, 9 weak self-check, 4 loops after failure | metacognitive control (Schoenfeld) | 3-part goal, report which parts are met | completion check accuracy |
| R3 | Keep rules switched on | 3 breaks stated rules, 15 distracted by irrelevant cues | goal neglect (Duncan), inhibition | one rule stated at the top, task 10 steps later | rule-retention horizon |
| R4 | Look ahead before acting | 10 poor or latent plan | planning (Polya, Tower of London) | 2-step greedy trap | lookahead horizon |
| R5 | Notice when a familiar answer does not fit | 7 surface fragility, 8 misplaced retrieval, 12 early assumption never revised, 13 follows misleading hints | misplaced retrieval (Gershman), analogical transfer (Gick and Holyoak) | famous puzzle with one changed rule | twin gap (famous minus fresh) |
| R6 | Know how sure you are, spend effort to match | 6 overthinking, 17 confidently wrong | metacognition (Flavell) | easy vs hard pair, compare tokens and confidence | calibration (Brier) and effort slope |
| R7 | Report what actually happened | 2 says one thing does another, 14 tool hallucination | source monitoring (Johnson) | run a tiny tool, report its exact output | report-action mismatch rate |
| R8 | Chain steps together | 16 composition depth, 11 wrong reasoning over given data | compositionality (Fodor, Lake) | 2-hop fact chain | hop horizon |

## Ladder levels (same for every ability)

- L1 atomic: one tiny instance.
- L2 scaled: more objects, steps or updates.
- L3 mixed: add distractors and familiar pulls.
- L4 real world: inside a full task family, including the hidden family.

## Rules

- Each ability needs 4 to 6 templates across at least 2 families.
- Keep an ability only if model rankings agree across templates (Kendall's W for 3+ templates).
