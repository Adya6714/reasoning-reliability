## Scope and count

This queue inventories the papers and reports named in the user's A–O list. ## Queue rules

- Start with the nine works in batch A, in order. Do one complete paper analysis at a time using the vault's current paper-note template (`research-vault/templates/NOTE.md` in the canonical repo). Stop after each note for the user's review before beginning another analysis.
- Before analysis, resolve the official paper/version and retrieve the LaTeX source when available. If no LaTeX source exists, record the accessible version and read the full paper PDF/text. Do not assume every preprint exposes source.
- Add a full note only after reading the paper. Until then, this queue is a discovery inventory, not evidence about the paper's methods or results.
- Record related works cited by a reviewed paper in a separate follow-up list only when they directly inform one of the project's questions or design choices; include the citation and why it merits reading.
